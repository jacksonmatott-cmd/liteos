The upstream JavaScript and WASM engine bytes are unchanged and SHA-256 checked
against oblivion-runtime-manifest.json. LiteOS wraps the documented shell
parameters, file-loading callback, print/error handlers, and temporary D: drive.
The local presentation.js adapter replaces both overlay presentation callbacks
with one lazy opaque 2D context, preserving crops, bitmap cleanup and frame
acknowledgements in finally. The direct GL surface and engine bytes stay intact.
Loader-only mode uses -novideo/-nosound and GDI. It does not test graphics/audio.
The complete wrapper and build source is in:
https://github.com/jacksonmatott-cmd/liteos

Dependency notices retained in the source archive include SDL (zlib license),
zlib/minizip, and other vendored libraries. Emscripten-generated glue uses the
MIT / University of Illinois-NCSA licenses; fsfs retains its MIT header.
https://github.com/emscripten-core/emscripten/blob/main/LICENSE
