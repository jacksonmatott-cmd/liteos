The upstream JS, WASM and .data bytes are verified against runtime-manifest.json
by the LiteOS build. A small declared JavaScript prefix scopes the engine's
origin-private file access to one LiteOS profile and library version. The
engine/game mechanics are unchanged. LiteOS configures local play and disables
the upstream online lobby. The complete integration source and reproducible
build scripts are in https://github.com/jacksonmatott-cmd/liteos.
